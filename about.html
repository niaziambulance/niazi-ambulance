Who We Are

Niazi Ambulance Service is a 24/7 ambulance transportation service based in Mianwali, providing reliable ambulance transportation for patients and families across Pakistan.

Our services include emergency ambulance transportation, ICU ambulance services, oxygen-supported transportation, patient transfers, dead body transportation and long-distance intercity ambulance services.

We focus on providing safe, reliable and professional transportation to help patients reach hospitals, healthcare facilities and other destinations.

Our Commitment

Our commitment is to provide dependable ambulance transportation whenever patients and families need it. We are available 24 hours a day, 7 days a week for emergency ambulance services, patient transfers and long-distance medical transportation across Pakistan.
