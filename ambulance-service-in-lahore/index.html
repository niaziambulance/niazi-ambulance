Need An Ambulance

Booking an ambulance in Lahore takes three simple steps

Whether it is a sudden emergency, a planned hospital transfer or an intercity move, our dispatch process is designed to get an ambulance moving toward you in minutes.

Step 1

Call or message us

Dial +92 305 9129038 or send your pickup address and patient condition on WhatsApp. Our team answers around the clock.

Step 2

We confirm & dispatch

We confirm the nearest available ambulance type — standard or ICU — and share the driver's contact and estimated arrival time.

Step 3

Ambulance arrives & assists

Our trained attendant helps move the patient safely, applies first aid if needed, and transports to the chosen hospital or destination.

In a life-threatening emergency, call us directly instead of typing — a phone call reaches our dispatch desk faster than a message.
